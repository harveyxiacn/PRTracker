**Contract-first parallelism:** before launching the developers, the lead wrote stub files with exported types and signatures (`throw new Error('not implemented')`). Each developer owned only its own files, plus a `src/i18n/parts/<module>.ts` strings file, so nobody edited a shared file at the same time. The lead merged the parts into `en.ts` and wired everything in. Developers verified their pure logic by hand-porting it to `.mjs` (no npm on the control host); the lead then ran tsc and vitest on the workstation.

**Translation deltas:** finished translator agents were resumed with a short written brief plus a JSON of new or changed keys (about 1 minute per language group). They also caught routing bugs in the lead's new intents, e.g. two handlers sharing the `documents` id.
