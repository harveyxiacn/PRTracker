PR Tracker — Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-16 · Applies to the Android app, the iOS app and the web app at harveyxiacn.github.io/PRTracker.

Summary

PR Tracker has no accounts, no servers that receive your data, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Data you enter

Your PR and status dates, trips, applications, documents metadata (type and dates — never document numbers), personal history, household members' names you choose, and assistant chats are stored only on your device (Android SharedPreferences, iOS UserDefaults, or your browser's IndexedDB). We cannot see, access or recover this data.

  • Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer of app data are disabled. Move your data with the app's own backup file, which you can encrypt with a passphrase.
  • On iOS, app data is included in your device's own encrypted backups if you enable them.
  • “Delete all data” in Settings removes everything from the device, including scheduled reminders.

Network requests

Permissions

  • Notifications — local reminders scheduled on your device; no push server.
  • Biometrics (optional) — to unlock the app; handled by your device; the app never receives biometric data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Not legal advice

PR Tracker provides estimates for planning. Always confirm with IRCC's official tools before applying.
