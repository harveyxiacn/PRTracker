PR Tracker — Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-16 · Applies to the Android app, the iOS app and the web app at harveyxiacn.github.io/PRTracker.

Summary

PR Tracker has no accounts, no servers that receive your data, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your device, unless you choose to share an anonymised timeline (see below).

Data you enter

Your PR and status dates, trips, applications, documents metadata (type and dates — never document numbers), personal history, household members' names you choose, and assistant chats are stored only on your device (Android SharedPreferences, iOS UserDefaults, or your browser's IndexedDB). We cannot see, access or recover this data.

  • Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer of app data are disabled. Move your data with the app's own backup file, which you can encrypt with a passphrase.
  • On iOS, app data is included in your device's own encrypted backups if you enable them.
  • Files you export (backups, CSV, calendar, reports) are kept in the app's private cache only while the share sheet is open, then deleted.
  • “Delete all data” in Settings removes everything the app stored on the device, including scheduled reminders and leftover export files. On the web it clears only PR Tracker's own browser database.
  • Data is not encrypted on the device; it is protected by the operating system's app sandbox and your device lock. The optional app PIN is a screen lock that stops others opening the app on your unlocked phone; it does not encrypt your data.
  • While an app PIN is set, reminder notifications on the lock screen say only “You have a reminder”, and the app is hidden in the recent-apps / app-switcher preview. Without a PIN, notifications include details such as days remaining and household members' names.
  • The web app is served from harveyxiacn.github.io, a domain shared with the developer's other GitHub Pages sites. Browsers separate stored data by domain, so for the most sensitive use we recommend the Android or iOS app.

Permissions

  • Notifications — local reminders scheduled on your device; no push server.
  • Biometrics (optional) — to unlock the app; handled by your device; the app never receives biometric data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Not legal advice

PR Tracker provides estimates for planning. Always confirm with IRCC's official tools before applying.
